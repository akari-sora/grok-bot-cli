Add `gbot migrate`: move a Grok Bot to Hermes, Muse or Your dot and check that it works there. `gbot migrate` alone (in a terminal) asks for the login, the bot(s) and the destination, previews the change, and after confirmation imports, reads the destination back and asks the migrated bot questions only the migrated data answers; `bots`, `capture`, `preview`, `import`, `check` and `run` are the same steps as commands. Capture is read-only (a backend-only session that never wakes the box) and complete: every transcript page (not a 200-message tail), side conversations, the bot's own, shared-user and project memory, the whole skill library, routines with schedules and time zones, todos and tasks, and the files the bot uses, each SHA-256 verified. Hermes gets a new profile per bot (SOUL, memory through Hermes' own stores, skill folders, paused cron jobs tagged with their routine, the whole history, workspace files), read back byte for byte. Muse and Your dot get a handoff folder on their own computer, sent by default as one zip attachment (`--via upload`; `--via chat` sends it file by file) through `trinity-cli migrate`, checked with a salted SHA-256 per part, then a long-term memory pointer; `--history`, `--skills` and `--folder` choose what goes. Sends are paced like the apps (one at a time, at least 20 s apart) and use each app's own request shape. Known token formats never leave the private archive; credential files are not read. The earlier `targets`, `inspect`, `export`, `plan`, `apply` and `verify` snapshot commands are replaced.
